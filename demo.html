Get Started with LabOps AI

Two ways to start

Most teams that reach out to us aren't shopping for another dashboard. They've usually just had a week where a critical specimen sat too long, a department fell behind without anyone noticing until it was too late, or a manager spent an afternoon manually piecing together a status report that should have taken thirty seconds. If any of that sounds familiar, you can get set up directly — or see it walked through first, whichever you'd rather do.

We don't run a generic pitch deck. We ask about your current workflow — how specimens move from collection to result, where you already feel friction, what tools you're stitching together today — and then we show the parts of the platform that speak directly to that. If AI risk prediction isn't relevant to your setup yet, we won't spend twenty minutes on it.

What happens once you sign up

  • Day 1: We connect your existing LIS and workflow data — no rip-and-replace, LabOps AI layers on top.
  • Week 1: Your team gets live dashboards and the AI risk monitor running against real specimens.
  • Ongoing: Predictions get sharper as the model learns your lab's specific patterns.

Prefer a walkthrough first?

If you'd rather see it in action before your team gets set up, we'll walk through the actual product against scenarios close to your lab — not slideware.

  • Minutes 0–5: A quick conversation about your lab — volume, departments, current pain points.
  • Minutes 5–20: A live walkthrough of the operations dashboard, AI risk monitor, and workflow view.
  • Minutes 20–30: Open Q&A — pricing, implementation timeline, data security, whatever's actually on your mind.
"We didn't need another reporting tool. We needed to know where our workflow was breaking down — and why."

No pressure, either way

No forced multi-week sales process. If it's not a fit, we'll tell you directly — and point you toward what we think would actually help instead.

Prefer to just talk first?

If you'd rather have a conversation before committing to a formal demo, our team is happy to do that too — reach out through our contact page and we'll go from there.
